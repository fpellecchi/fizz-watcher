## Before you send

- Replace every bracket. Delete any line that is not true for you.
- Put the old booking details high up and make them easy to check - name,
  email, reference, dates. A staff member who can verify you in 30 seconds
  is far more likely to help.
- Offer proof of the scam if you have it: the fake listing, the chat
  messages, the bank transfer. Evidence turns sympathy into a case they can
  justify acting on.
- Send it, then phone the building. Email joins a queue; a call reaches a
  person.
- Book the on-site viewing anyway. Staff tell people standing in front of
  them about rooms that never reach the website.
